<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
<p>Last updated: August 30, 2026</p>
<p>This Privacy Policy describes how Algup ("the App", "We", "Us") handles information when you use the App.</p>
<h2>Summary</h2>
<p>Algup is a Rubik's Cube algorithm trainer that runs entirely on your device. It does not have user accounts, does not use analytics or advertising services, and does not send any of your data to a server — because Algup does not have one. All app data stays on your device.</p>
<h2>Information We Collect</h2>
<p>We do not collect, receive, transmit, or have access to any of your data. Algup does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking services of any kind, and does not require you to create an account or sign in.</p>
<h3>Data Stored on Your Device</h3>
<p>Algup uses a local database (SQLite) stored only on your device to save your training progress and preferences, including:</p>
<ul>
<li>Your algorithm sets and cases, and any custom sets or algorithms you add</li>
<li>Your practice history and mastery progress for each case</li>
<li>App settings, such as your selected algorithm set</li>
</ul>
<p>This data never leaves your device. We have no access to it, and it is not transmitted to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the App deletes this data.</p>
<h3>Scramble Generation</h3>
<p>Scrambles are generated entirely on your device using an on-device solver. No cube state, scramble, or solve data is ever sent over the network.</p>
<h2>Third-Party Links</h2>
<p>The App contains links (for example, in the Settings and Credits screens) to external websites such as GitHub, Buy Me a Coffee, and third-party algorithm reference sites. Tapping these opens the site in your device's browser. We have no control over, and are not responsible for, the content or privacy practices of these external sites. Any information you choose to provide to them is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.</p>
<h2>Children's Privacy</h2>
<p>Algup does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the App has no accounts, no analytics, and no data collection of any kind, it can be used by users of any age without any personal information being gathered.</p>
<h2>Data Sharing</h2>
<p>We do not share, sell, or transfer any data, because we do not collect any. There is no server, database, or third-party service that receives information from the App.</p>
<h2>Security</h2>
<p>Since your data is stored only on your own device and never transmitted, its security depends on your device's own security (e.g. device passcode, encryption). We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date.</p>
<h2>Deleting Your Data</h2>
<p>You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling Algup, which removes its local database from your device. There is no remote copy for us to delete, since none is ever created.</p>
<h2>Changes to this Privacy Policy</h2>
<p>We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App's functionality changes to include network features. Any changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.</p>
